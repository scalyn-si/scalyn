Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the autonomous 5-agent swarm scout qualified prospects?

Agent 2 (Target Market Researcher) executes live internet searches across DuckDuckGo, Bing, LinkedIn, and community platforms using dynamic Boolean query matrices. It audits discovered websites for technical friction and detects concrete purchase intent signals with zero synthetic placeholders.

Is my startup brief, prospect list, and prompt history secure?

Yes. All discovered leads, competitor vulnerability analyses, and sales hooks remain strictly within your local environment. Data is persisted into your dedicated Master Excel database (master_lead_database.xlsx) with zero external telemetry leakage.

How does the Single Master Excel prevent duplicate contacts?

Our MasterExcelManager automatically fingerprints every discovered prospect using their Verified URL and entity name before appending. Duplicate records from repeat runs are safely skipped, and execution statistics are logged into a separate Run History sheet.

Can scalyn.si work for any startup industry or business model?

Yes. Our QueryPlanner and SignalDetector automatically adapt to B2B SaaS, local services, tuition coaching centers, boutique agencies, and D2C brands, formulating industry-tailored search queries dynamically.
